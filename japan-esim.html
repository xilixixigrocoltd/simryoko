Japan eSIM FAQ

Is my phone compatible with Japan eSIM?

Most phones released after 2018 support eSIM, including iPhone XS and later, Samsung Galaxy S20+, Google Pixel 3+, and many others. Check Settings → About to see if your device lists an EID number — if it does, you're compatible.

When should I activate the Japan eSIM?

You can install it before your trip, but activate it when you arrive in Japan. The plan validity starts from first use.

Can I make calls with the Japan eSIM?

Our Japan eSIMs are data-only. You can make calls via WhatsApp, LINE, or other VoIP apps over the data connection. Your primary SIM remains active for calls.

What networks does it use in Japan?

Our eSIMs connect to Japan's major LTE networks, providing nationwide coverage including all major cities and tourist destinations.

How do I top up if I run out of data?

Simply purchase a new plan from SimRyoko and apply it to the same eSIM profile — no reinstallation needed.
