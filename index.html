Common questions

Is my phone eSIM compatible?

Most phones from 2019 onwards support eSIM, including iPhone XS+, Samsung Galaxy S20+, Google Pixel 3+. Check your phone's settings under "Cellular" or "Mobile Data" for "Add eSIM".

When should I install the eSIM?

You can install the eSIM anytime after purchase, but activate it only when you arrive at your destination. Data validity starts from first use.

Will I lose my regular phone number?

No. The eSIM works alongside your physical SIM. You keep your regular number for calls and texts. The eSIM handles your data connection abroad.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) via Stripe, and USDT cryptocurrency (TRC-20). Instant confirmation, zero hassle.

How fast is delivery?

Instant. After payment is confirmed, the eSIM QR code is automatically sent to your email. Typically within 1–3 minutes.

What if the eSIM doesn't work?

We offer full support via AI chat and email. If there's a technical issue with activation, we'll replace or refund — no questions asked.
