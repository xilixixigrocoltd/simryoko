Thailand eSIM FAQ

Is my phone compatible with Thailand eSIM?

Most smartphones released after 2018 support eSIM, including iPhone XS and later, Samsung Galaxy S20+, Google Pixel 3+, and many others. Check Settings → About for an EID number — if present, your phone is eSIM-compatible.

When should I activate my Thailand eSIM?

You can install the eSIM profile before your trip, but only activate it when you arrive in Thailand. Your data validity starts from first use — not from purchase date.

Does it work on the islands — Phuket, Koh Samui, Krabi?

Yes. Our Thailand eSIM covers all major tourist destinations including Bangkok, Phuket, Chiang Mai, Koh Samui, Pattaya, Krabi, Ayutthaya, and most rural areas across the country.

Can I make calls with a Thailand eSIM?

Our Thailand eSIMs are data-only plans. You can make voice calls via WhatsApp, LINE, or FaceTime over the data connection. Your primary SIM remains active for standard phone calls and SMS.

What networks does the Thailand eSIM use?

Our Thailand eSIM connects to Thailand's major 4G LTE networks, providing excellent coverage across all provinces and tourist areas including islands and national parks.

How fast is the data connection in Thailand?

Thailand has excellent 4G LTE coverage in cities and tourist areas. Typical download speeds range from 20–80 Mbps — more than enough for streaming, video calls, and Google Maps navigation.

Can I use the eSIM on a tablet or iPad?

Yes, if your tablet supports eSIM. Compatible devices include iPad Pro (2018+), iPad Air (2019+), iPad mini 5+, and cellular iPads with eSIM capability. Check Apple's website for your specific model.
